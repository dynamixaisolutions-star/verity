🧊 ICE — free ice

🕳️ ECLIPSE — shadow trap
✓ VERIFY — boss challenge
☹ FALSY — lose a turn

Goal: reach FINISH first. Landing on VERIFY starts a cartoon boss challenge. Winning can give the MASK; the MASK makes you move 10% faster in this prototype. ECLIPSE sends you into a shadowy setback. “Die” sends you back to START. The handwritten rules have a few unclear numbers, so this version uses sensible defaults that can be changed later.
